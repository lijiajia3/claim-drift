# Scientific feasibility without new human raters

## Judgment

A defensible observational manuscript is feasible without additional raters if its primary question is changed substantively to the observed composition of citation text under an explicit selection rule. The current data cannot support a repaired test of belief updating, endorsement of the replicated finding, acknowledgment of failure or scientific self-correction. Calling the existing outcome a frozen model score prevents overinterpretation but is not, by itself, a new scientific contribution.

The most supportable question is: How does restricting an archived citation-text corpus to model-selected empirical restatements change the incidence and project-aligned comparisons of predefined visible word forms? The all-text denominator, identical-source comparison, exact selection decomposition, and source/project/timing checks now answer that question. The model-rated wording endpoints can be secondary descriptors of the conditional corpus.

This is a narrower empirical case study. Its general scientific value is that a stable rate of text retention does not establish stable representation of language within retained texts. The algebra is elementary and not claimed as a new method; the empirical result and transparent failure-inclusive support checks are the contribution. The fixed corpus, one filter, four selected projects and retrospective question choice leave meaningful novelty and generalizability risks for Scientometrics. The present improvements make the paper more coherent and auditable; they do not guarantee acceptance or justify presenting it as a general benchmark.

## Why the original semantic claim is not recoverable by renaming

The archived scoring instruction defines tentative modals as low scores and a categorical negative claim as potentially high. It also normally caps explicit failed replication, challenge or conflicting evidence at 0.25. The same asymmetry appears in the human annotation manual. A score can therefore decrease when a sentence discusses a replication challenge, remain high when it confidently rejects the original proposition, or refer to a different finding in a multi-study paper. There is no single monotone mapping from this scale to commitment to the original target or scientific correction.

The human data are a selected development set used in scorer choice. They do not independently establish the semantic construct or the target described by every citation. Adding raters under the same codebook would not remove that conceptual mixing. A future stronger semantic study would require a redesigned construct and target/context linkage as well as suitable evaluation labels. Lack of new raters is not a blanket barrier to an explicitly literal-form study.

Source-paper DOI identity and a reconstructed replication-test criterion are necessary but do not establish which proposition a citation discusses. Project publication dates are observable alignment conventions but do not identify a citing author's exposure. The archive's missing citing-paper/version IDs prevent verifying independence and article-level coverage. These limitations should remain short and explicit, rather than framing the entire manuscript as a list of anticipated future repairs.

## New material value added in this extension

The exact decomposition and influence analysis are newly computed, with a protocol fixed before those comparisons but after the earlier model and lexical results were known. They use all three unchanged lexical families and the same 72-source matched corpus. They show that the observed replication-word shift is mainly allocated to changes in the difference between retained and excluded marker incidence, with a smaller retention-share allocation. Its direction survives each single-source deletion; its magnitude and uncertainty depend materially on RPP dominance and window/support restrictions. The new report gives complete numerical results, including the weaker and interval-crossing specifications.

This is not an alternative estimate of the old latent certainty effect, a classifier accuracy score or evidence that the filter violated its own instructions. It is a comparison of two explicitly different observed text populations, with explanatory accounting of their difference. Avoid the term mechanism if it implies a causal process; use decomposition or composition instead.

## Existing external human annotations

Hardwicke et al. (2021), DOI https://doi.org/10.1177/25152459211040837, is particularly relevant related work. The public project and registered archive provide existing human article–original-study valence judgments under CC BY 4.0. The overlapping Carter and Caruso source cases could in principle provide a separate descriptive external audit, not certainty validation.

The available files do not presently support that audit. They contain citing DOI and Web of Science IDs but no general citation-context strings, while the present archive contains text and year without citing IDs. Selected counterargument quotations are not an unbiased replacement for the general text corpus. Cross-corpus linkage is consequently unassessed, not an estimated zero-overlap rate. No external row labels were examined. A metadata-only feasibility and gated evaluation proposal document this result separately. We do not collect a new corpus or unblind labels merely to force an external-validation claim.

## Recommended manuscript structure

1. Introduce the substantive selection problem in citation-context research and the specific observable comparison
2. Define all-text and selected denominators, fixed literal rules, source criteria and observable project milestones
3. Report every predefined family, all-text incidence, and identical-source all-text versus selected contrasts
4. Explain the replication-word contrast with the exact decomposition and immediately report RPP, five-year and excluded-text-support limits
5. Present the frozen score descriptors as a secondary view with concise measurement provenance; retain detailed legacy analyses and diagnostics in the supplement
6. Discuss what this purposive case study shows about conditional text descriptions, what it cannot establish, and how future studies should preserve citing identifiers and distinguish rhetorical commitment, stance and evidence discussion

## Related research and journal fit

Scientometrics has published studies of citance hedge-word incidence (Small, Boyack and Klavans, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11192-019-03016-z) and specified concept/uncertainty terms with explicit text-coverage limitations (Bornmann, Wray and Haunschild, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11192-019-03326-2). These support the legitimacy of carefully named literal observables, not transfer of their construct validity or data quality to this archive. Existing research on postreplication citation content, including Hardwicke et al., supplies the substantive setting and makes it important not to advertise a familiar observation or elementary accounting identity as a wholly new methodology.

Human-rater recruitment is therefore not the universal submission gate. The gates for the revised observational paper are accurate unit and denominator definitions, transparent post hoc provenance, full sensitivity reporting, reproducible calculations, and appropriately modest claims. Whether the bounded contribution is strong enough for this journal remains uncertain.
