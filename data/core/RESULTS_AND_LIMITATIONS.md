# Revised numerical results and remaining evidence needs

## Main comparisons

The repaired analysis retains a conservative baseline of105 source papers: 57 criterion-negative and 48 criterion-positive. Three source cases remain excluded under the conservative scope convention and are retained in sensitivity analyses. RPP73’s selected Staunton statistic is known; the remaining issue is broader site scope. The corpus contributes 15,280 text units and 8,427 model-filtered empirical-language units.

All differences below are criterion-negative minus criterion-positive, with equal source-paper weights and 19,999-resample source-bootstrap 95% intervals:

- Mean model-rated score: −0.00421 [−0.02175, +0.01339], 96 eligible sources
- Transformed lifetime drift: −0.00052 [−0.00718, +0.00631], 85 sources
- Project-journal-aligned change: +0.00306 [−0.02397, +0.02994], 72 sources

The event comparison uses RPP 2015, EERP 2016, ML1 2014 and ML3 2016 as journal-publication years. Recorded manuscript-upload timing, alternative text dates, omission of the event year and fixed symmetric windows are reported separately. Both criterion groups receive project results; the comparison is descriptive rather than an untreated-control design.

These intervals concern a frozen model score in a selected source-paper corpus. They do not establish unchanged wording, meaningful equivalence on a human certainty scale, or absence of scientific response.

## Precision

At the current event-analysis support, simulations stipulating a true +0.02 model-score difference detect it about 28.9% of the time under the stated empirical-variance assumptions. If the true difference is zero, equivalence within ±0.02 is established about 0.18% of the time. These are hypothetical operating characteristics, not achieved-power estimates or substantive equivalence bounds.

The illustrative TOST grid is explicitly post hoc. No retrospectively chosen smallest meaningful effect is asserted. Project-specific patterns and sparse-group uncertainty are retained rather than selected by statistical significance.

## Measurement

For the 330 ordinary annotation items, assertion agreement is κ=0.653. Among 248 ordinary items judged positive by both raters, ordinal certainty agreement is α=0.562. The denominators distinguish ordinary items, quality controls and repeated presentations. Retaining the first task-order presentation instead of the canonical ordinary record gives α=0.557.

The ensemble's development correlation with the two-rater mean is ρ=0.695, but the same 248 labeled items informed scorer selection. This is not held-out performance. Calibration and repeatability matter separately: 97 of 220 exactly linked human-positive texts received a different full-corpus ensemble score from their development-run score.

For actual full-corpus scores on 219 source-criterion-complete linked items, the differential post/pre model-minus-human error is +0.0733, with a wide source-cluster interval [−0.0357, +0.1728]. This selected-development-sample diagnostic neither establishes measurement invariance nor supplies an identified correction factor.

No new human labels were generated. A stronger claim needs independently qualified, target-conditioned relevance and stance judgments spanning criterion groups, periods and filter outcomes.

## Provenance and interpretation

The original archive retained text and year but omitted citing-paper identities and version keys. Consequently, the observed units are distinct text strings within source papers, not a verified census of independent citing articles. Multiple candidate dates and earlier-than-journal-year flags are preserved as uncertainty, not silently declared errors.

The selected replication-test criterion belongs to a particular target associated with a source paper. It does not validate every empirical statement later citing that paper. The repaired project labels, source criteria and year conventions improve the numerical analysis, while corpus-wide target alignment and independent human-scale validation remain unfinished.

The original results are preserved for comparison. The cleaned aggregate representation reproduces the repaired numerical results without distributing raw annotations, private receipt records or working reports.
